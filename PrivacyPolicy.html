Privacy Policy

Character Limit

Last updated: October 14, 2026

The short version

Character Limit does not collect, transmit, or share your personal information. Everything you type stays on your device. There is no account, no server, and no tracking.

What data we collect

None. We do not collect names, email addresses, device identifiers, usage analytics, crash reports, or advertising identifiers. There is no sign-up and no sign-in.

What stays on your device

The app stores the following on your device only, and it never leaves:

DataWhere it livesWhy
Text you type and copy On screen Counting characters. It is never written to disk.
Check history Application Support / history.json Your saved checks, so you can reopen them. Capped at 100 entries.
Your settings iOS UserDefaults Default limit, appearance, counting rule, and Pro status.
Purchase status Apple servers Apple's own receipt system, which we do not control or read.

You can erase all of it at any time: clear the history from within the app, or delete the app from your device. Both remove everything permanently.

Advertising

The free version shows an advertising placeholder. When advertising is enabled, the third-party ad provider may collect data as described in its own privacy policy, and Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework will ask for your permission before anything is tracked. We do not receive your personal data from advertising partners.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Apple. We never see or store your payment details. Apple handles all payment data and may ask you to confirm with Touch ID, Face ID, or your Apple Account password. Your purchase syncs across your devices through your Apple Account, not through an account on our servers.

Third-party services

We ship no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, and no advertising SDK. The app contains one third-party asset: the Inter typeface, bundled locally under the SIL Open Font License. Because it is bundled rather than fetched, your device never contacts a font server.

Children

Character Limit is not directed at children under 13. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, the app is safe for all ages.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct, or delete. You have full control over everything in the app through the settings and history screens inside the app itself.

Changes to this policy

If we add a feature that changes how data is handled, such as introducing analytics or a real advertising SDK, we will update this page and change the date at the top before the change ships.
